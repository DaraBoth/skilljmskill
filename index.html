3D on the web 3D លើវេបសាយ 14

The threejs skills are by CloudAI-X (MIT). 3d-web-experience is from vibeship-spawner-skills (Apache-2.0).

  • 3d-web-experience

    Helps you plan and build 3D on a website: a product you can turn, a 3D portfolio, or a page that moves in 3D as you scroll.

  • threejs-fundamentals

    The base of every three.js project: the scene, the camera, the renderer, the animation loop, resizing with the window, moving and grouping objects, and cleaning up memory when you are done.

  • threejs-geometry

    How to make shapes in three.js: built-in shapes, 3D text, your own meshes from points, lines and point clouds, and drawing thousands of copies fast with instancing.

  • threejs-materials

    How to choose and tune materials, the surface of a 3D object: basic, matte, shiny, realistic PBR, glass and cartoon toon materials, plus transparency and how each reacts to light.

  • threejs-textures

    How to use images on 3D objects: loading textures, the right colour space, UV maps, repeating and moving textures, cube maps, render targets and saving texture memory.

  • threejs-lighting

    How to light a three.js scene: the light types, shadows and how to fix shadow stripes, HDR environment light for real looking reflections, and keeping lights fast.

  • threejs-loaders

    How to load files into three.js: glTF and GLB models (also compressed ones), textures, HDR environments, showing a loading bar, and cloning or freeing models when you are done.

  • threejs-animation

    How to make things move in three.js: keyframes, skeleton animation for characters, morph targets for faces, playing the animations inside a glTF model, and blending two animations smoothly, for example walk into run.

  • threejs-interaction

    How to make a 3D scene react to people: click or hover on objects, drag them, move the camera with orbit controls, and turn mouse or touch positions into 3D positions.

  • threejs-shaders

    How to write your own shaders for three.js in GLSL: send values in with uniforms, move points in the vertex shader, colour pixels in the fragment shader, and change built-in materials.

  • threejs-postprocessing

    How to add screen effects after the 3D scene is drawn: glow (bloom), blur with depth of field, soft shadows in corners (ambient occlusion), outlines, colour grading and your own effects.

  • threejs-performance

    How to find out why a 3D scene is slow and fix it: measure first, then draw fewer objects with merging and instancing, use simpler models far away, lower texture memory and keep heavy work out of the animation loop.

  • threejs-asset-pipelineNode.js

    How to shrink 3D model files before you put them on a website, using gltf-transform: check a file for problems, compress the shapes with Draco or meshopt, compress textures to KTX2, WebP or AVIF, and set up the loader each format needs.

  • threejs-react-three-fiber

    How to use three.js inside React with React Three Fiber and drei: write the scene as JSX, use hooks for the animation loop and loading, handle clicks on 3D objects, and keep it fast.
